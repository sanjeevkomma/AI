# To Read
* The Generative Pre-trained Transformer (GPT) series of language models developed by OpenAI represents a significant advancement in the field of natural language processing (NLP) and artificial intelligence (AI)
* The series includes several iterations, each building on the advancements of its predecessor


# GPT Series Overview
# 1. GPT-1
Publication: June 2018
Model Size: 117 million parameters
# Key Innovations:
1. Transformer Architecture: Utilizes the Transformer architecture introduced in "Attention is All You Need," which leverages self-attention mechanisms to process text.
2. Unsupervised Pre-training: Pre-trained on a large corpus of text (BooksCorpus) to learn general language patterns.
3. Supervised Fine-tuning: Fine-tuned on specific tasks to adapt the model for various NLP applications.
# Impact:
1. Demonstrated the effectiveness of unsupervised pre-training followed by supervised fine-tuning
2. Achieved competitive results on several NLP benchmarks, showing the potential of transfer learning in NLP
