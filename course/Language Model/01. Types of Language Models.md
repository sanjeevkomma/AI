# Types of Language Models
# 1. Statistical Language Models
* Statistical language models are traditional models based on probabilistic approaches that use statistical methods to predict the likelihood of a sequence of words.
# Types:
* **N-gram Models:** These predict the next word based on the previous n words. Common types include:
    * Unigram: Considers only one word at a time.
    * Bigram: Considers two-word sequences.
    * Trigram: Considers three-word sequences, and so on.
