# To Read
* GPT = Generative Pretrained Transformer
* ChatGPT is based on the GPT-4 architecture, which is an iteration of the Generative Pretrained Transformer (GPT) series developed by OpenAI.
* GPT-4 is a large language model designed to understand and generate human-like text.
* It improves upon its predecessors, GPT-3 and earlier versions, by offering better performance in terms of understanding context, generating coherent and detailed responses, and handling more complex tasks across various domains.
