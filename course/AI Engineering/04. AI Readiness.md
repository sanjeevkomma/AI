# Definition
* AI Readiness is the state of having the strategic alignment, data maturity, technical infrastructure, and organizational capability to effectively integrate AI into business processes or decision-making
* AI Readiness refers to how well-prepared an organization, system, or individual is to successfully adopt, implement, and scale Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies

# Example AI Readiness Questions
* Do we have clean and centralized data?
* Do we understand which business problems AI can solve?
* Do we have leaders championing AI?
* Do we have the right platforms/tools (e.g., ML pipelines, MLOps)?
* Are employees trained to collaborate with AI systems?

# Why AI Readiness Matters
* Avoids failed or stalled AI projects.
* Maximizes ROI from AI investments.
* Prepares companies for competitive advantage.
* Ensures ethical and compliant use of AI.

# How to Improve AI Readiness
1. Start with a maturity assessment (e.g., Gartner, Deloitte frameworks).
2. Build a data strategy and modernize data infrastructure.
3. Upskill your teams in AI/ML, data literacy.
4. Establish AI governance frameworks.
5. Run pilot projects with business ROI tracking
